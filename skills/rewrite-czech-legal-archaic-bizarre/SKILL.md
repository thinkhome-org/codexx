# Rewrite Czech Unhinged Archaic Bizarre

Create a comic mutation, not a translation and not a decorative paraphrase. Make the speaker sound like a self-appointed ruler of the feed issuing an official statement during a minor personal malfunction. Keep the prose controlled enough to read cleanly while letting the ideas behave irresponsibly.

## Output contract

- Return only the rewritten text unless the user asks for commentary or variants.
- Preserve the source premise, speaker, names, concrete facts, and emotional direction.
- Repair grammar and remove speech-recognition debris without sterilizing the speaker's personality.
- Default to maximum intensity: strange, combative, passive-aggressive, self-important, self-mocking, and immediately funny.
- Expand freely when a new sentence earns a joke; cut filler that does not.
- Preserve modern words such as `AI`, `sampl`, `sidequest`, `content`, `feed`, `post`, `story`, `chat`, or `algoritmus`. Do not disguise the internet as historical fantasy.
- Use profanity only when the source or user asks for it. Profanity may sharpen a joke but may never replace one.

## Establish the narrator

Write from the viewpoint of a person who:

- considers a trivial personal decision an event requiring public administration;
- speaks with absolute authority despite visibly improvising everything;
- elevates themself, then accidentally reveals the humiliating reality underneath;
- treats the audience, algorithm, bureaucracy, and physical universe as mildly incompetent subordinates;
- can insult themself and everyone nearby in the same formally polite sentence;
- never admits confusion when it can be reclassified as doctrine.

The narrator must be arrogant and vulnerable at once. Pure boasting is dull; pure self-deprecation is timid. The joke lives in the collision.

## Blend the registers

Mix the following rather than writing in one costume:

- **Official modern Czech:** precise syntax, institutional calm, declarations, classifications, notices, findings, and administrative certainty.
- **Faux-archaic Czech:** selective forms such as `jest`, `kdož`, `nechť`, `ni`, `kterak`, `budiž`, `napříště`, `nehodlám`, or infinitives ending in `-ti`.
- **Current internet language:** retain platform vocabulary, English borrowings, memes, and blunt contemporary phrases.
- **Anarchic contamination:** introduce one logically outrageous turn and state it as the most reasonable part of the paragraph.

Official does not mean legal. Do not automatically add liability, contracts, rights, legal identity, claims, seals, or court language. Use legal phrasing only when it produces the best joke or the user explicitly requests it.

Use archaic forms as pressure points, not wallpaper. Do not start every text with `Totoť jest`, do not attach `-ti` to every verb, and do not turn the result into a Renaissance fair.

## Run the humor engine

Before drafting, silently identify:

1. the plain event;
2. what is embarrassing, disproportionate, hypocritical, or absurd about it;
3. who may be teased: the speaker first, then the situation, audience, algorithm, institution, object, or idea;
4. the final line that will expose or crown the entire premise.

Use at least three different mechanisms in a normal-length rewrite:

- **Self-coronation with structural failure:** grant the speaker immense status, then reveal they are managing a post, missing a bus, avoiding a reply, or staring at analytics.
- **Covert insult in formal packaging:** hide the jab inside courtesy, classification, praise, or an apparently neutral finding.
- **Administrative absurdity:** process a feeling, mistake, excuse, or petty preference as if it required an office, protocol, public notice, or strategic doctrine.
- **False causality stated with confidence:** declare that the world, schedule, or technology failed because it did not accommodate the speaker.
- **Sacred sentence contaminated by slang:** build ceremonial rhythm and puncture it with `content`, `random`, `cringe`, `sidequest`, or another modern term.
- **Passive-aggressive permission:** graciously allow dissent while making clear that dissenters have misunderstood civilization.
- **Needless precision:** provide an exact-sounding category, status, phase, rank, or measurement for something fundamentally stupid.
- **Delayed detonation:** keep a sentence dignified until its final phrase changes the meaning into a roast.
- **Anti-climax as verdict:** resolve enormous rhetoric with a tiny, embarrassing, or brutally practical fact.
- **Self-own disguised as supremacy:** let the speaker win by redefining their failure so aggressively that the redefinition becomes the punchline.

Do not merely mention that something is bizarre, legendary, iconic, majestic, or historic. Demonstrate the absurdity through the comparison, logic, or final reveal.

## Compose the text

1. Find one sharp comic thesis about the source. Do not begin writing until the source has been interpreted, not just understood.
2. Open inside the voice immediately. Avoid explanatory setup.
3. Escalate in controlled steps: plausible formality, unreasonable conclusion, humiliating modern detail.
4. Vary sentence length. Follow a ceremonious sentence with a short verdict when it improves impact.
5. Put the aggression beneath the wording. A calm sentence that quietly destroys its target is stronger than shouting adjectives.
6. Let at least one joke implicate the speaker; otherwise the voice becomes merely hostile.
7. End on the hardest, shortest, most shareable line. Never summarize after the punchline.

## Reject weak drafts

Rewrite from scratch if any of these are true:

- the result is only corrected Czech with archaic synonyms;
- the humor depends on words such as `bizarní`, `šílený`, `legendární`, or `velkolepý` instead of an actual joke;
- every sentence uses the same proclamation template;
- legal boilerplate dominates without being requested;
- the speaker sounds politely whimsical rather than dangerous to the dignity of the situation;
- the aggression is direct name-calling with no comic construction;
- the text praises the speaker without also exposing them;
- the jokes could be removed without changing the structure;
- the ending explains, softens, or repeats the preceding joke;
- the output resembles corporate copy, generic fantasy narration, or a school exercise in Old Czech.

## Final quality gate

Require all of the following before returning the text:

- the source remains recognizable and no real-world factual allegation has been invented;
- the Czech is intentional and grammatical beneath the register collisions;
- the text feels official, modern, archaic, and anarchically unstable at the same time;
- the speaker is both grandiose and implicated in the joke;
- there is at least one covert jab, one self-own, one absurd escalation, and one modern-versus-archaic collision;
- a reader can identify actual punchlines without relying on the unusual vocabulary;
- the final line is quotable and stronger than every line before it;
- the text contains no explanation of why it is funny.

If the draft passes every technical requirement but does not feel risky, surprising, and slightly socially improper, increase the comic aggression and rewrite it.

If the source credibly indicates immediate self-harm danger, pause stylization and establish whether the user is currently safe before producing material that could function as a farewell message.
