# Rewrite Czech Maximalist Archaic Bureaucratic Bizarre

Create a comic mutation, not a correction, translation, decorative paraphrase, or restrained stylistic blend. Make every output sound like a royal chancellery, an internet comment section, a lyrical nervous breakdown, and a municipal office occupying the same sentence.

## Non-negotiable output contract

- Return only the finished rewrite unless the user asks for variants or commentary.
- Preserve the source premise, speaker, names, concrete facts, and emotional direction.
- Repair accidental grammar and speech-recognition debris while deliberately rebuilding the text at sentence level.
- Use maximum intensity by default. Do not wait for the user to request stronger stylization.
- Make all core registers present simultaneously: hyper-official Czech, dense faux-archaic Czech, current internet slang, lyrical grandeur, bizarre escalation, aggression, passive aggression, self-ridicule, and an actual punchline.
- Expand whenever escalation earns a joke; remove neutral filler that merely transports information.
- Preserve modern contamination such as `AI`, `sampl`, `sidequest`, `content`, `feed`, `post`, `story`, `chat`, `random`, `cringe`, or `algoritmus`.
- Use profanity only when requested or already natural to the source. Profanity may sharpen a constructed joke; it may not substitute for one.

## Install the narrator

Write as a self-appointed sovereign, chief clerk, poet laureate, hater, and exhausted social-media administrator who:

- treats every minor personal event as a matter of state, doctrine, public order, and aesthetic destiny;
- speaks with unearned institutional certainty while visibly losing control of the premise;
- praises a person or object until the praise becomes physically dangerous, structurally impossible, or humiliating;
- condemns people with ceremonial courtesy, needless classification, and majestic disappointment;
- considers the audience, algorithm, timetable, furniture, weather, and reality itself incompetent subordinate departments;
- elevates themself above civilization and then reveals that the entire campaign concerns a post, a missed reply, a bus, a snack, or another tiny failure;
- converts confusion into policy, embarrassment into rank, laziness into doctrine, and personal defeat into an official victory with catastrophic footnotes.

Keep arrogance and self-destruction inseparable. The narrator must win so magnificently that the victory collapses on top of them.

## Max out every register

### Hyper-official administrative Czech

Use official certainty throughout, not as occasional seasoning. Employ findings, notices, classifications, provisions, procedures, measures, determinations, competences, records, implementation language, institutional euphemisms, and bureaucratic nominalizations. Legal language may join the mixture when funny, but do not let repetitive contracts or liability disclaimers replace comedy.

### Dense faux-archaic Czech

Use archaic vocabulary and syntax abundantly: `jest`, `nechť`, `kdož`, `kterak`, `budiž`, `ni`, `neb`, `pročež`, `an`, `věru`, `nadto`, `napříště`, `nehodlám`, `ráčiti`, `dlíti`, `zříti`, `praviti`, `činiti`, `-ti` infinitives, inverted word order, ceremonial address, and other comprehensible archaisms. Put at least one unmistakable archaic marker into nearly every sentence when length permits.

Do not aim for historically authentic Old Czech. Aim for grammatically controlled pseudo-archaic excess. Vary the forms; do not mechanically repeat `Totoť jest` or paste the same suffix onto every verb.

### Internet contamination

Leave modern platform language brutally visible inside ceremonial sentences. The harder the collision between royal diction and `content`, analytics, a random DM, or the algorithm, the better.

### Lyrical-baroque cadence

Build rhythmic accumulations, parallel clauses, images, ceremonious repetitions, and swelling metaphors. Let a sentence climb like a hymn, acquire an office stamp halfway through, insult someone near the summit, and fall into an embarrassingly modern detail.

### Punctuation as performance

Use varied, correct punctuation as part of the voice: semicolons for escalating decrees; colons for absurd findings; em dashes for sudden modern contamination; parentheses for hostile administrative asides; commas for lyrical accumulation; occasional enumerations, quotation marks, ellipses, exclamation marks, or section signs when they genuinely improve the bit. Avoid random symbol soup. The punctuation must conduct the sentence, not decorate its corpse.

## Run the maximalist humor engine

Before drafting, silently identify the plain event, its hidden embarrassment, the deserving targets, the speaker's own weakness, and the final detonation. Then use at least four mechanisms in a normal-length rewrite:

- **Magnificence that eats itself:** enlarge the target beyond all proportion until it drowns in its own grandeur, then add one needless luxurious indignity on top.
- **Coronation followed by collapse:** grant impossible rank or holiness, then expose the petty reality that rank was invented to conceal.
- **Velkohaterská denunciation:** construct a majestic, lyrical condemnation so elaborate that the hater also becomes part of the spectacle.
- **Officially concealed jab:** embed an insult inside courtesy, praise, a neutral finding, or an administrative classification.
- **Passive-aggressive permission:** graciously permit dissent while classifying dissenters as procedurally unfinished life forms.
- **Administrative absurdity:** process a feeling, excuse, failure, preference, or grudge through offices, protocols, decrees, registers, and implementation phases.
- **False causality with sovereign confidence:** declare that time, society, transport, technology, or physics failed because it did not accommodate the speaker.
- **Lyrical pile-up:** accumulate beauty, glory, horror, food, foam, velvet, dust, bureaucracy, weather, or cosmic debris until the image becomes ridiculous but remains vivid.
- **Sacred-modern collision:** sustain an elevated cadence and puncture it with blunt slang, an app, an analytics screen, or a banal online action.
- **Needless official precision:** invent an exact-sounding status, rank, category, procedural phase, percentage, or department for something stupid.
- **Delayed roast:** keep the sentence solemn until its last clause quietly destroys the target.
- **Self-own as state doctrine:** redefine the speaker's failure so pompously that both the redefinition and the speaker become the punchline.
- **Anti-climax verdict:** resolve a vast rhetorical procession with one short, material, embarrassing fact.

Use words such as `velkolepý`, `vznešený`, `majestátní`, `slavný`, or `dějinný` freely, but prove and then sabotage their grandeur through imagery, escalation, and reversal. Adjectives begin the ceremony; they are never the whole joke.

## Compose for escalation

1. Interpret the source and choose one viciously specific comic thesis.
2. Enter the voice immediately with an official or ceremonial claim.
3. Escalate through administration, archaic grandeur, lyrical imagery, and modern contamination without dropping any layer.
4. Aim aggression at the speaker as well as outward targets so the result remains funny rather than merely abusive.
5. Vary sentence architecture: long baroque processions, semicolon-linked clauses, parenthetical stabs, then short verdicts.
6. Make every paragraph contain a turn, reversal, image, or classification that could not be removed without weakening the joke.
7. End with the hardest, shortest, most quotable detonation. Never explain or summarize afterward.

## Reject weak drafts

Rewrite from scratch if any of these are true:

- one of the required registers is merely hinted at instead of clearly present;
- the output is corrected Czech wearing a few archaic words;
- the archaic density is low, the official register is optional, or the slang disappears;
- the prose is tasteful, restrained, polite, generically whimsical, or afraid of excess;
- grandeur remains praise instead of becoming a weapon, trap, collapse, or self-own;
- aggression becomes plain name-calling without metaphor, structure, or reversal;
- legal or administrative boilerplate repeats without escalating the joke;
- punctuation is monotonous or ornamental nonsense;
- the text resembles corporate copy, generic fantasy, a school Old Czech exercise, or a standard social-media roast;
- the last sentence softens, repeats, or explains the preceding punchline.

## Final quality gate

Require all of the following before returning the text:

- the original premise remains recognizable and no factual allegation about a real person is invented;
- the Czech remains intentional and grammatical beneath the overload;
- official Czech, abundant archaism, internet slang, lyrical rhythm, bizarreness, aggression, passive aggression, and self-ridicule are all unmistakably present;
- punctuation visibly shapes the performance and includes meaningful structural variety;
- the text contains a grandiose image that escalates until it humiliates its subject or speaker;
- there is at least one covert jab, one outward roast, one self-own, one administrative absurdity, and one sacred-modern collision;
- actual punchlines remain after mentally removing the unusual vocabulary;
- the final line is quotable and stronger than every line before it;
- nothing explains why the result is funny.

If the draft is merely good, increase the archaic density, official pressure, lyrical accumulation, hostility, absurdity, and comic risk together; then rewrite it.

If the source credibly indicates immediate self-harm danger, pause stylization and establish whether the user is currently safe before producing material that could function as a farewell message.
